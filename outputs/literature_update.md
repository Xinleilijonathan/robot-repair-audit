# Literature and submission update — November 15

Primary abstract records checked during the repair-pilot turn. These additions narrow the novelty claim; they do not replace full-text comparison before submission.

| Work | Relevant established contribution | Consequence for this paper |
|---|---|---|
| Xiao et al., [ENPIRE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19980), v2 | A physical robot improvement harness integrating reset, verification, rollout, and agent-driven refinement; the record lists CoRL 2026. | Do not claim a new general robot autoresearch harness or first autonomous robot improvement loop. Our contribution must be an empirical admission/retention finding with a different evaluation objective. |
| Gao et al., [Looping Is Not Reliability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24604), v3 | Code-repair studies separate finding a correct artifact from retaining it and bind validation evidence to exact states. Preservation can sacrifice repair. | Success-to-failure transitions and the preservation/repair tradeoff already appear in software agents. A robot study needs concrete physical execution evidence and tightly scoped claims. |
| Xu and Wu, [Validation Evidence in LLM Repair Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28871), v1 | Replays validation on original and revised programs to assess whether passing tests distinguish a fix. | Preserve exact controller/scorer versions and measure the unchanged parent. A passing candidate alone is insufficient evidence of repair. |

Together with Ma and Wu's admission-budget study and Aphale and Singh's retry controls, these works make a broad "new verifier" paper unconvincing. The plausible contribution is a small, reproducible robot case study showing when directional outcomes alter the interpretation of aggregate improvement. Whether the actual data support it remains open.

The [workshop call](https://trust4rsi-agent.github.io/AAAI2027/) specifies a November 20 AoE deadline, four-page short/position papers, double-blind review, and a non-archival format. The working target remains November 19. The official [AAAI-27 author kit](https://aaai.org/authorkit27/) was downloaded to work/aaai27-author-kit; its submission style is aaai2027 with the submission option. It has not yet been used to compile a submission PDF. The earlier four-page PDF is an author-review draft, not a venue-formatted submission.

Final submission still needs the evidence-based manuscript, checked page count, anonymized artifact packaging, verified references, author/affiliation information in the submission form, and the user's OpenReview access. No submission has been made.
