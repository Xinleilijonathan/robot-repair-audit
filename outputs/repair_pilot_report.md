# Repair pilot report (development evidence)

Written November 15, 2026, ~15:00 America/Los_Angeles, after all three queued batches completed. Every number below comes from these files: `robot_repair_study/results/{radial_candidate_a, radial_candidate_b, radial_parent_repeat}`, `pilot_comparison.json`, `pilot_diagnostics.json` and `scene_table.json`. They were regenerated by `compare_pilot.py`, `pilot_diagnostics.py` and `scene_table.py`. **All of this is development data**: the scenes were found by adaptive search, the target was chosen from them, and the proposals were written after inspecting its failure. None of it is a final audit, and none of it estimates how often failures occur.

**What the gains and losses consist of:**

- **Old gains come mostly from scenes with the target's failure mechanism.** A gained 9 of its 10 old labels, and B 12 of its 13, on the other r80 scenes, which fail the same LIFT way as the target. The remaining gain for each candidate is the parent's 0.031 m near-miss. So "old gains" here means generalizing the repair to the same failure, not unrelated improvement.
- **Every loss is a placement-precision regression.** The cube was lifted, released, retreated and stable, but settled 0.034–0.040 m from the goal. These are not drops or collisions.

## 3. Placement margin and tolerance sensitivity

**Margin shift.** This is the paired change in final placement error on the 44 old parent successes:

| | Median Δerror | Worse by >5 mm | Better by >5 mm | Max worsening |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | +1.1 mm | 9 | 4 | +27.6 mm |
| B | −1.0 mm | 5 | 13 | +21.6 mm |

A erodes placement margin more often than it improves it. B mostly tightens placement. The single B loss is a +21.6 mm outlier on one reset.

**Tolerance sensitivity.** This re-scores outcomes at other tolerances. It is approximate because it uses the final-frame error with all other v2 predicates kept; at 0.030 m it reproduces the frozen labels on 150/150 checked rollouts. It is a sensitivity analysis, not an alternative result.

| Tolerance | A old loss / gain | B old loss / gain |
|---|---|---|
| 0.020 m | 8 / 14 | 10 / 17 |
| 0.025 m | 8 / 10 | 1 / 14 |
| **0.030 m (frozen)** | **4 / 10** | **1 / 13** |
| 0.035 m | 4 / 12 | 1 / 12 |
| 0.040 m | 0 / 12 | 0 / 12 |

The gate disagreement holds from 0.020 to 0.035 m and **disappears at 0.040 m**. Any claim about it is therefore conditional on a precision requirement near 3 cm. The paper must state this.

## 4. Parent reproducibility (reset/order diagnostic)

The discovery parent (two original folders, `radial_parent` and `boundary_parent`, kept as separate runs) was compared with the repeated parent label by label. Result: **75/75 identical outcomes** (44 both succeed, 31 both fail, 0 transitions). The maximum final-error difference was 2.2×10⁻⁵ m, and step counts were identical on every label.

Within one process, the three resets of a scene are *not* identical: 9 of 25 scenes have differing repeat outcomes or errors. The ordered rollout sequence is therefore reproducible across processes, even though consecutive resets carry small state differences.

Consequences:

1. Every candidate transition in §2 is a deterministic consequence of the edit under this ordered sequence, not reset noise.
2. Reusing parent results across candidates is justified when the scene order is identical.
3. The three repeats of a scene are not independent samples. The effective development sample is 25 scenes, and 1 repair context.

## 7. Limitations of this pilot

- **One target context and two proposals.** The proposals were interactive Codex assistance with full conversational context: no frozen model, no sampling configuration, no resampling. This is a feasibility case, not an agent benchmark.
- **Single failure mechanism.** Both edits address the same boundary LIFT failure. Their old gains are mostly that same failure elsewhere on the ring.
- **Losses are small.** They are ~1 cm precision regressions near the tolerance and vanish at 0.040 m.
- **Diagnostic gates only.** The full-panel gate decisions use 75 labels per configuration, which is not the planned 24-rollout budget-matched validation.
- **Narrow setting.** It is the stock scripted Franka controller, one cube, one place goal, no obstacles and CPU physics.

## 8. Go / no-go decision

The pilot met the protocol's conditions for going further:

- Real repairs were observed (2/2 proposals repaired the target, 3/3 each).
- The comparison is informative: the target improved, the old losses were offset by old gains, and the full-panel gates disagreed for both candidates.
- The parent is fully reproducible across processes, so pairing is clean.

**Decision:**

- **GO** for one **frozen, independent audit of this single repair context**: candidates A and B against the parent on freshly sampled scenes. It includes a budget-matched validation draw on which gate decisions are committed *before* the audit batches run. The scope is a case study.
- **NO-GO** for the planned 10-context × 2-candidate main study before the November 19 target. There is only one failure mechanism, no independently sampleable proposer, and the time would go to new failure discovery that would itself be adaptive.

The audit is specified in `robot_repair_study/audit_protocol.md` and is frozen, with hashed manifests, before any audit rollout. Paper type: an evidence-backed short case-study paper, with the claims bounded as in §7.
